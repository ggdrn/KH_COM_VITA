# KH:COM Vita v0.08.00

**European ROM support (German, French, Italian, Spanish), boss practice, new upscalers and 16:9 menus.**

## New

- **European ROM:** the same VPK runs the USA or the European version (English, French, German, Italian,
  Spanish), detected from your ROM. Each version keeps its own saves (`khcom_eu*.sav` for Europe).
- **Boss practice** (options menu → **BOSSES**): refight any boss you have beaten in Sora's story, at its
  original strength or at an enemy level from 1 up to Sora's. No XP or rewards; win or lose, you return to
  where you were with the HP you had. Available while exploring a world's rooms.
- **Tutorial replay** (**TUTORIALS** tab): the basics tutorial and Leon's combos/sleights tutorial.
- **Smooth edges: Scale2x and MMPX** added next to Scale3x. MMPX keeps the pixel art's outlines. Choose
  whether they apply to everything, sprites only or scenery only.
- **Clear stocked cards:** hold the rear touch pad (0.5, 1, 1.5 or 2 s) or tap it twice.
- **16:9 layouts** for the pause, save and door menus and the level-up screen; wide menus also for the
  world map, map/world cards and the journal.

## Fixed

- Crash when opening the pause menu after the first fight with Riku.
- Leon's tutorial could get stuck if the stocked cards were cleared or the hand ran out.
- Slowdown in heavy scenes: drawing and upscaling are split over three cores.
- Pressing Start with the menu chord (Start + L + R) no longer opens the game's pause menu too.

## Installation

1. `libshacccg.suprx` at `ur0:data/libshacccg.suprx`
   ([guide](https://samilops2.gitbook.io/vita-troubleshooting-guide/shader-compiler/extract-libshacccg.suprx)).
2. Recommended: [kubridge](https://github.com/bythos14/kubridge/releases) in `ur0:tai/config.txt` under
   `*KERNEL`.
3. Install `khcom_v0.08.00.vpk`.
4. Copy your own ROM dump to `ux0:data/khcom/` (`rom.gba` or any `.gba` name):
   - USA: SHA-1 `10729bd884f8fdca7a310b6d606c52e46657aa48`
   - Europe: SHA-1 `8db73586cdb11b3795907edebf43228dbcd3e6b2`

   With both in the folder, `rom.gba` is used, otherwise the USA one.

Existing saves and `config.ini` keep working. **This VPK contains no game data.**

## Known issues

- Boss practice is not available in Reverse/Rebirth (Riku's story) yet.
- The European version is new: please report any cut or misplaced text, with `ux0:data/khcom/log.txt`.
- Smooth edges cost CPU time; in heavy fights (e.g. Ursula) prefer Scale2x or off.
