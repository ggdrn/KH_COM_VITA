# KH:COM Vita v0.08.01

**Crash fixes (Genie/Jafar in Agrabah, Riku on 12F), boss practice in Riku's story, staff roll music and
widescreen cutscenes without black edges.**

## Fixed

- **Crash at the Genie's entrance before the Jafar fight** in Agrabah.
- **Crash in a fight against Riku on 12F** after skipping the scene before it.
- **Staff roll music** now plays.
- **Cutscenes no longer show black edges** in widescreen: scenes framed at an end of their map (Monstro,
  Halloween Town, Wonderland, Agrabah, Hollow Bastion, Destiny Islands, castle halls...) keep the camera
  a little further inside, and scenes on a narrow background are stretched to the whole screen.
- Smoother video: the frame is no longer re-uploaded to the GPU the slow way every frame, and frames
  that did not change are not drawn again.

## New

- **Boss practice in Reverse/Rebirth:** Riku's bosses as his story gets past them (with his full Castle
  Oblivion deck), and the other story's bosses once it is finished.
- **EXTRAS tab** (was TUTORIALS): Sora's tutorials and the staff roll of either finished story.

## Installation

1. `libshacccg.suprx` at `ur0:data/libshacccg.suprx`
   ([guide](https://samilops2.gitbook.io/vita-troubleshooting-guide/shader-compiler/extract-libshacccg.suprx)).
2. Recommended: [kubridge](https://github.com/bythos14/kubridge/releases) in `ur0:tai/config.txt` under
   `*KERNEL`.
3. Install `khcom_v0.08.01.vpk` (over v0.08.00 is fine).
4. Copy your own ROM dump to `ux0:data/khcom/` (`rom.gba` or any `.gba` name):
   - USA: SHA-1 `10729bd884f8fdca7a310b6d606c52e46657aa48`
   - Europe: SHA-1 `8db73586cdb11b3795907edebf43228dbcd3e6b2`

   With both in the folder, `rom.gba` is used, otherwise the USA one.

Existing saves and `config.ini` keep working. **This VPK contains no game data.**

## Known issues

- Smooth edges cost CPU time; in heavy fights (e.g. Ursula) prefer Scale2x or off.
- Please report crashes with `ux0:data/khcom/log.txt` and the crash dump from `ux0:data/` (`psp2core-*.psp2dmp`).
